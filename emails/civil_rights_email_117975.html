John Lewis, to introduce the Equality Act, a landmark bill that extends crucial non-discrimination protections to LGBT individuals. The Equality Act amends the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and other existing laws to prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity in the areas of employment, education, credit, housing, federal funding, jury service, and public accommodations. Our society is built on the principle that all are created equal. Discrimination of any kind is not only harmful to each one of us, but at its most basic level, harmful to our democracy. No American should be evicted from a home, fired from a job, or bullied in school because of who they are. I will continue to stand with my colleagues in defense of equality for all Americans and will continue this fight until discrimination of all kinds is eradicated in our country. Working for a Long-term Solution to Fix our Crumbling Roads Due to years of neglect, our roads highways and bridges are in severe disrepair. For example, as of 2012, 536 of Colorado's bridges were deemed unsafe for public use and 40 percent of our roads were evaluated as in poor condition. Yet as money to repair old highways and build new ones lapses in six days, House Republicans are pushing for another short-term patch to a long-term problem. I stand ready to commit, along with my Democratic colleagues, to a long-term plan to invest in the future of our economy and rebuild our roads and bridges. It's time Republicans step up and commit to long-term planning as well. We can't afford to keep kicking this can down the road. Constituent Success Story Today, I am proud to be able to share a recent story about how my office helped someone in need. After over 30 years of service with the federal government, Paul, a former Post Office employee retired with the promise of retirement pay. Unfortunately, he did not receive retirement checks when they were due. After three months he tried to contact the Office of Personnel Management (OPM), the agency that distributes retirement benefits for federal employees. When he did not hear back about the status of his retirement checks, he contacted my office for assistance. My office was able to get in touch with OPM and resolve the issue. Paul is now receiving his retirement pay earned during many decades of service.
